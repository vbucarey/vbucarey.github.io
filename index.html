Víctor Bucarey

Associate Professor · Universidad de O’Higgins

I develop models and algorithms for better decision making, bringing together mathematical optimization, machine learning, and game theory.

My research focuses on bilevel optimization, decision-focused learning, and large-scale computational problems. I hold a Ph.D. in Systems Engineering from the University of Chile and enjoy sharing research through science outreach.
